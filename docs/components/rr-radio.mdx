## Overview

`rr-radio` is one option inside an `rr-radio-group` — it renders the dot-and-label pair and reports selection to its parent. It must not be used standalone: the group owns the name, the submitted value, and the selection state; a lone radio has no way to be deselected and no form participation. Give each radio a distinct `value`; disable individual options with `disabled` (the group's `disabled` kills the whole set).
